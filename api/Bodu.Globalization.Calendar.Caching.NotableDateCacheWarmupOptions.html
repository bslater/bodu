    Class NotableDateCacheWarmupOptions

    Configures the startup cache warm-up registered through AddNotableDateCacheWarmup: the territories to warm and the span of civil years they are warmed over.

    Remarks

    The warmed span defaults to a rolling window around the current UTC civil year — [year − YearsBehind, year + YearsAhead], evaluated when the warm-up runs — so a long-lived deployment always warms the years users actually query. Either bound can be pinned with FirstYear or LastYear; a pinned bound replaces its rolling default independently of the other.

    Properties

    Name Description
    FirstYear

    Gets or sets the fixed inclusive first civil year of the warmed span, replacing the rolling YearsBehind start.
    LastYear

    Gets or sets the fixed inclusive last civil year of the warmed span, replacing the rolling YearsAhead end.
    Territories

    Gets the territory codes to warm.
    YearsAhead

    Gets or sets the number of civil years after the current UTC year the warmed span reaches forward, used when LastYear is not set.
    YearsBehind

    Gets or sets the number of civil years before the current UTC year the warmed span reaches back, used when FirstYear is not set.
