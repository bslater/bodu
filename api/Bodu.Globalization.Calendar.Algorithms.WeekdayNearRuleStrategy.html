    Class WeekdayNearRuleStrategy

    Calculates a weekday on, before, after, or nearest to the date produced by another rule, such as the first Monday after Easter Sunday.

    Remarks

    This is the dynamic-reference counterpart to WeekdayNearDateStrategy: the anchor is another rule's occurrence rather than a fixed month and day. The referenced rule is resolved for year + referenceYearOffset, letting a rule in one civil year anchor to a reference in an adjacent year. A missing, ambiguous, circular, or recurring reference produces no occurrence and is reported by semantic validation where statically detectable. 

    // The first Monday after Easter Sunday.
var strategy = new WeekdayNearRuleStrategy("easter-sunday", null, DayOfWeek.Monday, WeekdayProximity.After);
