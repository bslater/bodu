    Enum WorkingDaysOfWeek

    Represents a named pattern of working days within a seven-day week.

    Namespace: Bodu
    Assembly: Bodu.Core.dll
    Syntax
    public enum WorkingDaysOfWeek
    Remarks

    WorkingDaysOfWeek enumerates the commonly observed working-week patterns across global regions. Each named value maps to a canonical WeekPattern preset and is interchangeable with the bitmask representation through the conversion extension methods on WorkingDaysOfWeekExtensions.

    Use a named value when the working week is one of the listed patterns; use Custom together with a caller-supplied WeekPattern when the working week is non-standard (for example a four-day week or an unevenly distributed schedule).

    Fields

    Name Description
    MondayToFriday

    Indicates a Monday through to Friday working week (Western standard).
    MondayToSaturday

    Indicates a Monday through to Saturday working week.
    MondayToThursdayAndSaturday

    Indicates a Monday through to Thursday, and Saturday working week.
    SaturdayToThursday

    Indicates a Saturday through to Thursday working week (e.g. parts of the Middle East).
    SaturdayToWednesday

    Indicates a Saturday through to Wednesday working week.
    SundayToFriday

    Indicates a Sunday through to Friday working week.
    SundayToThursday

    Indicates a Sunday through to Thursday working week (e.g. Israel, parts of the Middle East).
    Custom

    Indicates a non-standard working week whose pattern is supplied externally via a WeekPattern.
    AllDays

    Indicates that every day of the week is treated as a working day (no weekend is defined).
