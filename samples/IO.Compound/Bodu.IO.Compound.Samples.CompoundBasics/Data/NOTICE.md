# Sample data provenance

`sample1.doc` is a Word 97-2003 document from the test corpus of
[`waveform-computing/compoundfiles`](https://github.com/waveform-computing/compoundfiles)
(`tests/`), redistributed under the MIT terms below (Copyright (c) 2014
Dave Jones). It is byte-identical to
`Bodu.IO.Compound/test/Fixtures/Reference/valid/sample1.doc`, whose provenance
is recorded in
[`Bodu.IO.Compound/test/Fixtures/Reference/NOTICE.md`](../../../../Bodu.IO.Compound/test/Fixtures/Reference/NOTICE.md).

`golden-v3.cfb` is generated by Bodu. It is a copy of the writer golden
`Bodu.IO.Compound/test/Fixtures/Writer/golden-v3.cfb`, which
`CompoundStorageBuilder` writes from the canonical tree in
`CompoundStorageBuilderSerializationTests.Golden.cs`.
