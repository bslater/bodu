Remarks

BiffReader is a ref struct and cannot be stored in a field. A consumer that reads incrementally - a class that walks a substream one record per call, or a loop feeding chunks of a Stream - captures CurrentState after each pass, slices its buffer at BytesConsumed, and constructs the next reader from the remaining bytes and the captured state. The record position itself is not part of the state: it is expressed by where the caller slices the buffer.
