    Enum AdjustmentTrigger

    Identifies the condition that determines whether an adjustment policy applies to a calculated occurrence.

    Namespace: Bodu.Globalization.Calendar
    Assembly: Bodu.Globalization.Calendar.dll
    Syntax
    public enum AdjustmentTrigger

    Fields

    Name Description
    Always

    The adjustment always applies, regardless of the calculated occurrence.
    IfDayOfWeek

    The adjustment applies when the calculated occurrence falls on one of the policy's configured weekdays.
    IfWeekend

    The adjustment applies when the calculated occurrence falls on a weekend: a day outside the resource's working week (Saturday or Sunday by default).
    IfWeekday

    The adjustment applies when the calculated occurrence falls on a working day within the resource's working week (Monday through Friday by default).
    IfNonWorkingDay

    The adjustment applies when the calculated occurrence falls on a non-working day: a day outside the resource's working week, or a day already claimed by another non-working occurrence.
    IfWorkingDay

    The adjustment applies when the calculated occurrence falls on a working day within the resource's working week that is not claimed by another non-working occurrence.
    IfLeapYear

    The adjustment applies when the calculated occurrence falls in a Gregorian leap year.
    IfBeforeFixedDate

    The adjustment applies when the calculated occurrence falls before the policy's comparison month and day in the same year.
    IfAfterFixedDate

    The adjustment applies when the calculated occurrence falls after the policy's comparison month and day in the same year.
    IfNthOccurrenceInMonth

    The adjustment applies when the calculated occurrence is the configured nth weekday of its month.
    Custom

    The adjustment applies when a caller-supplied IAdjustmentTriggerHandler, bound through the policy's trigger handler key, reports that it should fire.
