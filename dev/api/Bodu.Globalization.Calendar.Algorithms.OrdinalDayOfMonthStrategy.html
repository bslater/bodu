Remarks

A positive ordinal counts from the start of the month (1 is the first day); a negative ordinal counts from the end (-1 is the last day). The result always stays within the specified month: a positive ordinal that does not exist in a particular year and month produces no occurrence. 

// The last day of February (28 or 29 depending on the year).
var strategy = new OrdinalDayOfMonthStrategy(2, -1);
