Remarks

SpecVersion selects the grammar the reader enforces. The default, V1_0 , is the most widely deployed released specification; V1_1 additionally accepts the TOML v1.1.0 grammar features.

MaxDepth bounds the nesting of tables and arrays the reader will materialize, guarding against stack-exhausting input. A value of 0 selects the default of 64, and a larger value is clamped to the hard ceiling Bodu.Text.Toml.TomlLimits.AbsoluteMaxDepth; a document that nests deeper than the effective limit throws TomlFormatException rather than risking a StackOverflowException.
