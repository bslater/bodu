Bodu.Globalization Namespace

Purpose

Bodu.Globalization is a namespace root: it holds no types of its own. Its namespaces cover dates and schedules:

  • Bodu.Globalization.Calendar — the notable-date engine (Bodu.Globalization.Calendar), with its regional data bundles, caching, and dependency-injection packages declaring into the same namespace.
  • Bodu.Globalization.Recurrence — RFC 5545 recurrence rules, cron expressions, and anchored intervals (Bodu.Globalization.Recurrence).
  • Bodu.Globalization.Extensions — date and calendar extension methods that ship in the Bodu.Core package.
