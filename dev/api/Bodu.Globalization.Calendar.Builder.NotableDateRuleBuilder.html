Provides a fluent surface for authoring a single rule: its selection scalars, applicability scope, exactly one calculation strategy, rule-specific tags, and the adjustment policies applied to its occurrences.

Examples

// U.S. Thanksgiving: the fourth Thursday of November, scoped to the United States.
definition.AddRule("us", r => r
    .ForTerritory("US")
    .DayOfWeekInMonth(11, DayOfWeek.Thursday, WeekOrdinal.Fourth)
    .AsNonWorking());

Remarks

A rule must declare exactly one calculation strategy - for example Fixed(int, int, bool, bool), DayOfWeekInMonth(int, DayOfWeek, WeekOrdinal), WeekdayNearDate(int, int, DayOfWeek, WeekdayProximity), OffsetFromRule(string, int, string?), or Algorithm(string). Scope the rule to a calendar, territories, or a year window with the For… / …Year(s) members, and attach reusable adjustment policies with WithAdjustment(string). Configure the builder inside AddRule(string, Action<NotableDateRuleBuilder>).
