    Class WorkingDayInMonthStrategy

    Calculates the Nth working day from the beginning or end of a Gregorian month, such as the first working day of January or the last working day of a reporting month.

    Remarks

    A positive ordinal counts working days from the start of the month (1 is the first working day); a negative ordinal counts from the end (-1 is the last working day). Working days exclude rest days and applicable non-working notable-date occurrences, computed deterministically and independently of resource-declaration order by the StrategyResolutionContext. When the requested ordinal does not exist within the month, no occurrence is produced rather than spilling into an adjacent month. 

    // The first working day of January.
var strategy = new WorkingDayInMonthStrategy(1, 1);
