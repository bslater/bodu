Remarks

The warmed span defaults to a rolling window around the current UTC civil year — [year − YearsBehind, year + YearsAhead], evaluated when the warm-up runs — so a long-lived deployment always warms the years users actually query. Either bound can be pinned with FirstYear or LastYear; a pinned bound replaces its rolling default independently of the other.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

Thrown when Territories is empty or contains a blank code, when YearsBehind or YearsAhead is negative, when a fixed year is outside the representable calendar range, or when FirstYear and LastYear are both set and inverted.
