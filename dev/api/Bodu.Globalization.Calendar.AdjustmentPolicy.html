Remarks

A policy composes a Scope that limits where it applies, a Trigger that decides whether it fires, an Action that computes the observed date, and an Emission mode that decides which occurrences are emitted. Policies are referenced by rules through stable ids.

Working-day actions honour SkipWeekends and SkipNonWorkingDates: a day is skipped while seeking the observed date if it is a weekend (when SkipWeekends is set) or already claimed by another non-working occurrence (when SkipNonWorkingDates is set). The latter is what advances a substitute past a day already taken by another holiday.

Remarks

The IfNonWorkingDay and IfWorkingDay triggers also depend on whether another non-working occurrence claims the date, which this method cannot determine alone; they are evaluated by the resolving service and never fire here.
