Remarks

Returning this metadata alongside the rate gives the caller everything required to explain which observed value was selected (provider, date, inversion direction, distance from the requested date, and policy applied) without having to re-query the underlying table.

Provenance is always populated. A rate resolved directly by a provider carries Live with a null Backend, CachedAtUtc, and Age. A rate served from a cache carries Cache with the serving backend, the instant the served data was cached, and the age it had at the lookup instant.
