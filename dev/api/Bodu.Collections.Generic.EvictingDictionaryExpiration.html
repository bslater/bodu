Remarks

Supplying an EvictingDictionaryExpiration to an EvictingDictionary<TKey, TValue> constructor enables the time dimension of the cache: entries can carry a time-to-live after which they become invisible to lookups and enumeration, independently of the capacity-triggered EvictingDictionaryPolicy. When no expiration configuration is supplied, the dictionary performs no clock reads and behaves exactly as a capacity-only cache.

TimeToLive is the default lifetime applied to entries added without a per-entry override. When null, entries added through Add(TKey, TValue) or the indexer never expire; only entries added through the time-to-live overloads ( Add(TKey, TValue, TimeSpan) and TryAdd(TKey, TValue, TimeSpan)) carry a lifetime.

Kind selects how lifetimes are measured. Under Absolute an entry expires TimeToLive after it was added or last updated. Under Sliding every successful read access - TryGetValue(TKey, out TValue), the indexer getter, and ContainsKey(TKey) - restarts the countdown; enumeration and Touch(TKey) do not.

TimeProvider drives all clock reads, enabling deterministic testing with a fake time source. It defaults to System.
