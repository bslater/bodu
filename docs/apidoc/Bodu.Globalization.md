## Purpose

**Bodu.Globalization** is a namespace root: it holds no types of its own. Its namespaces cover dates and schedules:

- <xref:Bodu.Globalization.Calendar> — the notable-date engine (**`Bodu.Globalization.Calendar`**), with its regional data bundles, caching, and dependency-injection packages declaring into the same namespace.
- <xref:Bodu.Globalization.Recurrence> — RFC 5545 recurrence rules, cron expressions, and anchored intervals (**`Bodu.Globalization.Recurrence`**).
- <xref:Bodu.Globalization.Extensions> — date and calendar extension methods that ship in the **`Bodu.Core`** package.
