## Purpose

**Bodu.Collections** is a namespace root: it holds no types of its own. Its namespaces span two packages:

- <xref:Bodu.Collections.Generic> - the specialized generic collections (**`Bodu.Collections`**), with the thread-safe variants in <xref:Bodu.Collections.Generic.Concurrent> (**`Bodu.Collections.Concurrent`**) and the collection extension methods in <xref:Bodu.Collections.Generic.Extensions> (**`Bodu.Core`**).
- <xref:Bodu.Collections.Specialized> - special-purpose collections such as the packed bit set (**`Bodu.Collections`**).
- <xref:Bodu.Collections.Probabilistic> - approximate sketches: Bloom filter, count-min sketch, HyperLogLog (**`Bodu.Collections`**).
- <xref:Bodu.Collections.Extensions> - non-generic enumerable extension methods (**`Bodu.Core`**).
